Privacy

During focus sessions FocusGuard records which sites you visit and for how long, never page contents, form inputs or messages. Page titles are collected only if you enable them in the dashboard, and never on banking, health, email or messaging sites. Blocking also works in incognito if you allow it on the extension's details page.
